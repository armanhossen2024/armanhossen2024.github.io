# Research

## Overview

My research focuses on the development, calibration, and scientific application of advanced X-ray and gamma-ray detectors for space-based astrophysics. I work at the intersection of instrumentation, data analysis, and high-energy astrophysics, contributing to next-generation missions that probe extreme cosmic environments such as black holes, neutron stars, and gamma-ray bursts.

My work combines experimental detector physics, systems integration, and computational methods to enable precise measurements of high-energy photons and to advance our understanding of the dynamic universe.

Current Research: X-ray Detectors and CubeSat Missions
As a Postdoctoral Researcher at Penn State, I lead efforts in the calibration and integration of hybrid CMOS X-ray detectors for the NASA-funded BlackCAT CubeSat mission. This mission is designed to detect high-energy transients and rapidly trigger follow-up observations from ground- and space-based observatories.

My current research activities include:

**Detector Calibration & Characterization**
- Measurement of quantum efficiency, gain, read noise, and energy resolution
- Event grading and performance optimization for hybrid CMOS detectors

**Instrumentation Development**
- Operation of high-vacuum and cryogenic test systems
- Development of precision temperature control systems for detector stability

**Automation & Data Pipelines**
- Design of Python-based pipelines for instrument control, data acquisition, and spectral analysis
- Implementation of statistical methods for uncertainty propagation and performance validation

**Mission Integration**
- Collaboration with multidisciplinary teams on instrument integration, calibration campaigns, and flight readiness

This work directly supports the development of next-generation space-based X-ray observatories.


## Ph.D. Research: TES Detectors and Balloon Missions
During my PhD at Washington University in St. Louis, I focused on transition-edge sensor (TES) gamma-ray microcalorimeters, developing and testing detectors for balloon-borne astrophysics missions such as DR-TES.

Key contributions include:

**TES Detector Development**
- Cryogenic operation and calibration of superconducting microcalorimeters
- Integration of detector arrays with a mini dilution refrigerator

**Balloon Missions**
- Contributions to X-Calibur and XL-Calibur hard X-ray polarimetry missions
- Calibration and performance validation of CdZnTe (CZT) detector systems

**Data Analysis & Modeling**
- Development of signal-processing pipelines for detector optimization
- Characterization of instrument response and noise properties
  
These efforts supported multiple NASA-funded missions and resulted in peer-reviewed publications.
