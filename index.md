# Md. Arman Hossen

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Department of Astronomy and Astrophysics at Pennsylvania State University. My research focuses on X-ray and gamma-ray detector development for space astronomy, including Hybrid CMOS detectors, TES microcalorimeters, cryogenic detector systems, and calibration pipelines for NASA missions.

## Research Interests

- X-ray and gamma-ray detector development
- Hybrid CMOS X-ray detectors
- TES microcalorimeters
- Cryogenic instrumentation
- High-energy astrophysics
- NASA balloon and CubeSat missions

## Current Work

I currently work on the NASA-funded BlackCAT CubeSat mission, a soft X-ray coded-aperture telescope designed to detect high-energy transients such as gamma-ray bursts and provide rapid alerts to ground-based observatories.
