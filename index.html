Md. Arman Hossen

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Department of Astronomy and Astrophysics at Pennsylvania State University. My research focuses on X-ray and gamma-ray detector development for astrophysics missions, including Hybrid CMOS detectors, Transition-Edge Sensor (TES) microcalorimeters, cryogenic instrumentation, and calibration pipelines for NASA missions.

Research Interests

  • X-ray and gamma-ray detector development
  • Hybrid CMOS X-ray detectors
  • TES microcalorimeters
  • Cryogenic instrumentation
  • High-energy astrophysics
  • NASA balloon and CubeSat missions

Current Work

I currently work on the NASA-funded BlackCAT CubeSat mission, a soft X-ray coded-aperture telescope designed to detect gamma-ray bursts and electromagnetic counterparts to gravitational-wave events.
